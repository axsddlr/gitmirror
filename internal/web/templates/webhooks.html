Webhooks

Webhooks make the copies update within seconds of a push, instead of waiting for the next scheduled copy. They are optional: without them everything is still copied on schedule.

Public address

The site sends webhooks to this address, so it must be reachable from the site. For GitHub that means from the internet. It must lead to gitmirror's webhook port (server.listen, now {{$d.Listen}}), not to this page's port.
